## Status language

- **Not recorded** — no reliable evidence of meaningful study yet.
- **Covered** — discussed/studied previously; recall and application still need checking.
- **Practised** — you attempted explanations, problems, or exercises.
- **Review** — a known gap, misconception, or uncertainty needs revisiting.
- **Mastered** — demonstrated accurate recall and application across **at least two fresh cases**, including a later follow-up.

Statuses can be combined, e.g. `Practised · Review`.
