# Visuals — WSET Level 3 Self-Study Pack

Infographics and schematic maps for the gradaran WSET Level 3 tutor pack.
All files are self-contained SVG (no external images or fonts), dark text on a
light background, with large labels. Open any `.svg` directly in a browser.

> **Note on maps: all maps in `maps/` are schematic — not to scale, relative
> positions only. They show where regions sit in relation to each other, not
> precise borders, distances or shapes. Do not use them for navigation or for
> exact geography.**

## Accuracy notes

- Burgundy is **continental**, not maritime — stated in `climate-types.svg`,
  `maps/burgundy.svg` and `maps/france-wine-regions.svg`.
- Malic acid falls sharply with ripening; tartaric acid is more stable; lactic acid
  comes from malolactic fermentation, not from the grape — stated in
  `grape-composition.svg` and `winemaking-flow.svg`.
- Prädikat is ripeness at harvest, not sweetness alone — stated in `sweetness-terms.svg`.
- No invented dates, figures or geography. Where a figure is a commonly cited
  approximation (e.g. qvevri in Georgia, "around 8,000 years, as commonly cited"),
  it is labelled as such in `history-fun-facts.md`.
